Grifos Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Grifos Studio is the app studio of Panagiotis Delis, an individual developer based in Stockholm, Sweden. Grifos Studio is the data controller for the apps listed below. You can contact us at grifosstudio@gmail.com.

This policy covers all Grifos Studio apps on Google Play, including:

  • Foldoku
  • Wordfold
  • MultiTap – Times Tables
  • Beamline
  • Stack Up

We built our apps to collect as little information as possible. We don't ask for your name, email address or any account, and we don't sell personal data.

Information we collect

Information stored by the apps

Your game progress, settings and scores are stored on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. Foldoku and Stack Up use Google Play Games Services for online leaderboards. If you are signed in to Play Games, Google shares your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and your scores with the game so they can appear on the leaderboards. We don't receive your email address, and we don't run our own leaderboard servers. You can manage your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our free apps show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location (country or region)
  • device information such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app's privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases, such as removing ads, are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock the feature.

Legal basis

We process data for ads based on your consent (personalised ads) or our legitimate interest in funding free apps (non-personalised ads). Purchase data is processed to fulfil the purchase you made.

Children's privacy

MultiTap – Times Tables is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. In MultiTap:

  • we don't collect any personal information from children
  • ads are child-directed and never personalised, and come only from Google Play Families-certified ad providers (Google AdMob)
  • there are no leaderboards, chat or social features
  • the remove-ads purchase goes through Google Play, which requires a parent's or account holder's authorisation according to the family's Google account settings

We handle data in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR's rules for children. Our other apps are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we store almost nothing ourselves, most data can be removed by uninstalling the app or resetting your advertising ID. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings.

To exercise your rights, email grifosstudio@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or your local data protection authority.

Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the app. Leaderboard scores are stored by Google Play Games according to Google's policies. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app or on its store listing.
